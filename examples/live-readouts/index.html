Flowdot — live readouts

a readout name:blended node binds to a store counter and renders it as a live number. The feed is split by a weighted pick (@0.75 blend / @0.25 drop); the three meters tick up as count received/blended/dropped run. No host JS — the numbers come straight from the behaviour store.
