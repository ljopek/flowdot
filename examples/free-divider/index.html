Flowdot — free divider / rule

divider "label" x1,y1 -> x2,y2 (alias rule) draws a standalone labelled line — a process boundary or a hot/cold split — that no note or zone could express. Dashed by default; add solid for a plain rule and a #colour to tint it. No host JS.
