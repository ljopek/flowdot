pub/sub tiered — conflation & when-dirty pollers

A publisher updates each tier's last-value slot fast; the & fan-out broadcasts every publish to all subscribers. Each tier polls at its own rate and consumes when dirty(slot). Writing a slot a slow tier hasn't read yet supersedes it — that update is conflated. Probe document.querySelector('canvas').__flowdot.rt.store (see superseded, readFast/readMed/readSlow).
