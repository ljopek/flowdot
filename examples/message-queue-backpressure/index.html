message-queue backpressure — drop-if, modes, queue depth

Each tick a producer is chosen with pick p in [p1, p2] and enqueues onto a bounded queue (the ring's fill is the live depth). On arrival drop if queue >= 12 sheds load when full; the consumer drains one per tick. Toggle burst below to make the producer outrun the consumer and watch the ring pin full and start dropping.
